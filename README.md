# Homework Hub

All your Brightspace, Gradescope and Pearson deadlines in one list, on your laptop and your phone.

**Latest version: 1.9.2** · [Download](https://github.com/blossqt/homework-hub-releases/releases/latest)

## Install

**Windows**

1. Download `HomeworkHub.exe` from the [latest release](https://github.com/blossqt/homework-hub-releases/releases/latest) and keep it somewhere of your own, such as Documents.
2. Open it. Windows may say it protected your PC, because the app isn't code-signed. Choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. The dashboard opens and Homework Hub sits in the tray. Settings walks you through adding your Brightspace calendar, the browser extension and your phone.

**Android**

1. On your phone, download `HomeworkHub.apk` from the [latest release](https://github.com/blossqt/homework-hub-releases/releases/latest) and open it. Allow installing apps from your browser when Android asks.
2. In the app, enter the address shown on your laptop under **Settings → Connect a phone**.

**Browser extension** (for Gradescope and Pearson)

The Windows app keeps a copy of the extension up to date. In Edge or Chrome, open the extensions page, turn on **Developer mode**, choose **Load unpacked** and pick the folder shown in the laptop app's Settings.

## Updates

Everything updates itself. The Windows and Android apps check for new versions and offer to install them, and the extension reloads itself when your laptop has a newer one. The Windows app only installs updates carrying the release signature, and Android only installs an update signed like the app it replaces.
